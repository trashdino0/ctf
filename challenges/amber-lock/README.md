# Amber Lock

**Category:** Knowledge / Paleontology  
**Difficulty:** Medium → niche  
**Points:** 400

Twelve specimen locks release twelve fragments of an archive index. The
fragments arrive scrambled; identify each specimen, then assemble the pieces by
their index codes to recover the flag.

This is a knowledge challenge with a Jurassic Park control-room skin. The early
locks are famous names; later locks require formation names, anatomical terms,
nomenclatural rules, and obscure transitional taxa. Web research is allowed and
expected. Pop-culture myths are corrected in the field notes rather than
presented as facts.
